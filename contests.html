Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Buying anything does not improve your chance of winning. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals who are 18 or older (or the age of majority in their place of residence) in jurisdictions where the contest is lawful. Market.Place staff, contractors and sponsors' employees, and their immediate families, are not eligible.
  3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the first day of the calendar month at 00:00 to the last day at 23:59 Eastern Time.
  4. Judging. These are skill-based contests, not lotteries. A panel of Market.Place editors scores entries on usefulness to other sellers (40%), clarity and evidence (30%) and originality (30%). Judges' decisions are final.
  5. Prizes. Prize values are shown in USD and paid by digital gift card or PayPal within 30 days of the winner confirming. Market.Place may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Prizes aren't transferable. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Canadian residents must correctly answer a time-limited skill-testing question to claim a prize.
  6. Notification. Winners are notified by email and must reply within 14 days or an alternate winner may be chosen.
  7. Licence. You keep ownership of your entry. By entering you grant Market.Place a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to publish it (with your first name and country, if you agreed) for editorial and promotional use.
  8. Conduct. Entries must be your own, truthful, and free of personal data belonging to others. We may disqualify plagiarised, fraudulent or offensive entries.
  9. Privacy. Entry data is used only to run the contest. See our privacy policy.
  10. Not sponsored by any platform. These contests are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by eBay, Etsy, Amazon, Meta or any other marketplace mentioned.
