Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. The first plan is free and comes with no obligation. If you want hands-on help afterwards (listing rewrites, store setup, a marketplace build), we'll quote it clearly or introduce you to a vetted specialist. You decide.

Who reads my request?

A Market.Place editor who works on marketplace strategy every day. Not a bot, and not a call centre.

Will you sell my details?

No. We never sell or rent contact details. We only make an introduction to a partner if you say yes first.

How fast will I hear back?

Within two business days, usually sooner. Complex marketplace-launch requests may get a short follow-up question first.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We cover North America, the UK, Europe, India, Australia and global cross-border selling.
