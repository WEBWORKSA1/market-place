How the match is scored

Each platform has an editorial fit profile: the product types its buyers actively shop for, the price ranges that sell well, whether it supports local pickup, how much ongoing work it takes, and which kinds of sellers it suits. Your answers add or subtract points on each dimension. Availability in your country is weighted heavily, because a perfect platform you can't sell on is useless. Our reach, fee and ease scores break ties.

No platform pays to be ranked here, and the quiz has no affiliate links. If the result surprises you, read the profiles and run your numbers in the fee calculator. Most successful sellers end up on two or three platforms. The choosing a marketplace guide explains how to cross-list without burning out.
