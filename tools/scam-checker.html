Rules that stop almost every scam

  1. Keep messages and payment inside the platform, where purchase protection applies.
  2. Never share a verification code, password or banking login.
  3. Never pay with gift cards, wire transfers or crypto for marketplace items.
  4. Meet in public, ideally a police-station exchange zone, for local deals.
  5. Don't refund an "overpayment" until the original payment has fully cleared, and better still, cancel it.
