    How this calculator works

    Each platform's fees are stored as simple rules: a percentage, a fixed amount, and what the percentage is charged on (the item price alone, or the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays). Some rules only kick in within a price band, like a flat fee under a threshold and a percentage above it. The calculator adds up every rule that applies to your price, subtracts the total from what the buyer pays, then takes off your own costs.

    What's included and what isn't

    • Included: selling or referral fees, transaction fees, payment processing and per-order fixed fees, where the platform publishes them.
    • Not included: optional promoted-listing ads, monthly subscriptions (shown as a note), currency conversion, sales tax or VAT the platform collects from buyers, and category exceptions beyond the general rate.

    Why profit is lower than you think

    The fee line most new sellers miss is the percentage charged on shipping. If a platform charges its fee on the order total, a $10 shipping charge costs you more than $10. The other one is packaging: boxes, mailers, tape and labels quietly cost $0.50 to $2 per order. Put both in and your margin is honest.

    Use the reverse mode before you buy inventory

    Switch to Price to hit a profit, enter the profit you need per sale, and the calculator finds the lowest list price that gets you there after fees. If that price is above what similar items actually sell for, don't buy the inventory. Pair it with the flip ROI calculator and our pricing guide.

    Fees change. Every rule shows the date we verified it and links to the platform's official fee page. Check that page before you make a big decision, and if you spot a change before we do, tell us.
