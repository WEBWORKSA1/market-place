The three numbers that matter in a flip

ROI tells you how hard your money is working. Many full-time resellers aim to at least double their cost on low-priced items, because fees, returns and dead stock eat the difference. Effective hourly rate tells you whether you're running a business or just keeping busy. Lot profit matters when you buy in bulk: one great item can carry a box of duds, but only if you price the great one properly.

Use real sold prices, not asking prices, for your expected sale price. Our reselling business guide walks through sourcing, comps and scaling, and the fee calculator shows every fee line for any platform.
