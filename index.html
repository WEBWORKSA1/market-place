About Market.Place

Is Market.Place a place to buy and sell?

No. Market.Place is an independent guide. We help you choose where to buy and sell, understand the fees, and stay safe. You list and buy on the platforms themselves.

Where does your fee data come from?

From each platform's official fee and help pages. Every rule shows the date we verified it and links to its source. You can edit any rate in the calculator.

Do marketplaces pay you to rank them?

No. Scores and recommendations are editorial and never sold. We earn money from clearly labelled ads, sponsorships and the optional Growth Desk.

Is it free?

Yes. Every tool, profile and guide is free, with no account needed. Nothing you type into a calculator leaves your browser.
