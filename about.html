About & methodology

We're the referee, not a player

Market.Place is an independent publication that helps people choose, compare and succeed on online marketplaces. We don't run a marketplace, take a cut of your sales, or let any platform buy a better score.

Why we exist

Selling online has never had more options, or more fine print. Fee structures change several times a year, a fee charged on shipping can quietly halve a thin margin, and scams change faster than help pages do. We built Market.Place to put the numbers, trade-offs and warning signs in one place, in plain language, for free.

Methodology

Fee data

Every fee rule in our calculators comes from the platform's official fee or help pages. We record the date we checked it and link to the source. We re-verify on a quarterly cycle and whenever a platform announces a change. Where a fee varies by category, we use the general rate and say so. You can override any rate in the calculator to match your category or account.

Editorial scores

Each profiled platform gets four 1–10 scores. They're judged against the same criteria on every platform:

  • Reach: the size and buying intent of the audience a typical seller can reach.
  • Low fees: total seller cost on a typical sale (10 = cheapest).
  • Ease: time and skill needed to list, sell, ship and get paid.
  • Protection: how clear and fair seller and buyer protection programmes are.

Scores are editorial judgments, not measurements. We explain the reasoning in each profile.

The Marketplace Match quiz

The quiz scores platforms on an editorial fit profile: the product types its buyers shop for, workable price bands, regional availability, local pickup, workload and seller type. Availability in your country weighs most.

Independence and how we make money

  • Advertising: Google AdSense ads, always labelled and kept apart from tool results.
  • Sponsorships: labelled sponsor slots in the newsletter, contests and directory. Sponsors never influence scores, rankings or fee data.
  • Growth Desk: optional consulting and, with your permission, introductions to vetted specialists.
  • Reader support: voluntary contributions via our support page.

We currently use no affiliate links in rankings or tool results. If that ever changes, links will be clearly disclosed and rankings will stay editorial.

Editorial standards

Guides are written from hands-on selling experience and checked against official sources. Specific figures link to their source. When we get something wrong, we fix it and note the change. Spotted an error? Send a correction.

Who's behind it

Market.Place is run by a small, remote team of sellers, researchers and writers. We're always looking for people who have sold online and care about accuracy. See careers.
