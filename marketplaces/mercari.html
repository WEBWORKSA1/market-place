Mercari: fees, pros, cons & selling tips

A simple app for shipping secondhand goods anywhere in the US, from clothes to collectibles.

At a glance

  • Mobile-first resale app covering almost every category, not just fashion.
  • One flat 10% selling fee, with no separate payment processing fee for sellers.
  • Buyers pay a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on top of the price.
  • Prepaid shipping labels can be generated in the app, or you can ship on your own.
  • Standard bank transfers are free; Instant Pay costs $3.

Who it's best for

Mercari suits people who want to list quickly and ship nationwide without learning a complex seller dashboard. It handles a broad mix of items: clothing, sneakers, toys, video games, small electronics, home decor and trading cards. Casual sellers like that the fee is easy to understand, and resellers use it as one of several channels for cross-listing.

It is a weaker fit for bulky items that are costly to ship and for luxury goods where buyers expect a dedicated authentication service.

Because every sale ships, Mercari also works for people who do not live near a large city, where local classifieds have fewer buyers. A seller in a small town can reach shoppers nationwide with the same effort it takes to post a local ad.

What it costs to sell

Mercari's current US structure, in place since January 6, 2025, is straightforward for sellers. You pay a flat 10% selling fee on the final item price plus any shipping the buyer pays. There is no additional payment processing fee on the seller side, and standard direct deposit withdrawals are free (one per day). If you want your money within minutes, Instant Pay costs $3 per transfer.

Buyers pay their own fee: a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on the item price and shipping. This does not come out of your earnings, but it does raise the buyer's total, which is worth remembering when you set prices. Mercari has also tested tiered fee discounts for some high-volume sellers, so check your account for any offer.

Worked example ($50 item, you offer free shipping):

  • Selling fee: 10% × $50 = $5.00
  • You receive: $50.00 − $5.00 = $45.00, minus the cost of the shipping label you cover.
  • The buyer separately pays 3.6% × $50 = $1.80 as a protection fee, so they pay $51.80 plus any tax.

If the buyer pays $8 shipping instead, the fee base becomes $58, and your fee is $5.80.

Fees verified October 2026 on Mercari's official fee structure help article.

Strengths

  • Simple pricing. One percentage, no listing fees and no processing add-on.
  • Fast listing. Snap photos, pick a category and the app suggests details.
  • Broad categories. Not limited to fashion, so it works for mixed inventory.
  • Shipping integration. Prepaid labels and weight-based options make shipping easy.
  • Free standard payouts. No charge for ordinary bank transfers.

Watch-outs

  • Fees on shipping. The 10% applies to buyer-paid shipping as well.
  • Fee changes. Mercari has revised its fee model several times in recent years; check before you price.
  • Offer culture. Buyers frequently send low offers, and price-drop notifications encourage waiting.
  • Payment timing. Funds are released after the buyer rates the transaction or the rating window passes.
  • Return claims. Disputes over condition can result in returns you did not plan for.

Tips to sell more here

  1. Use all available photo slots, with the first photo clean and well lit, and include close-ups of any flaws.
  2. Write a title that leads with brand and item type, then size, model or color, since buyers search by those terms.
  3. Lower your price in small steps over time; price drops can notify buyers who liked your item.
  4. Respond to offers with counteroffers instead of ignoring them; many buyers will meet in the middle.
  5. Weigh items before listing so you choose the right shipping tier and avoid overweight charges.
  6. Ship within your promised window and upload tracking promptly to keep ratings strong.
  7. Bundle related items, such as accessories with a main product, to raise your average sale.

Buyer protection in brief

Mercari holds the buyer's payment until the order is delivered and the buyer confirms everything is as described, or the rating window passes. If an item does not arrive, arrives damaged, or does not match the listing, buyers can report the problem and Mercari reviews the case. Buyers pay a protection fee for this coverage. Mercari also has seller protection policies for certain situations, but sellers should still document item condition with photos and use tracked shipping.

If a buyer reports a problem, respond in the order messages with facts and photos rather than arguing. Many disputes end with a return, so pack items in a way that lets you check their condition when they come back. For electronics and collectibles, a short video of the item working or being packed can be valuable evidence. Avoid accepting payment or arranging delivery outside the app, since those transactions are not covered.
