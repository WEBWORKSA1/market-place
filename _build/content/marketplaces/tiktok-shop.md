## At a glance

- Products are sold directly inside TikTok through videos, live streams, a shop tab and product pages.
- TikTok's official category table lists a 6% referral fee for most US categories.
- Some jewelry sub-categories and pre-owned categories are 5%.
- Creator affiliate commissions, set by the seller, are a major extra cost.
- Optional Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) handles storage and shipping for a fee.

## Who it's best for

TikTok Shop works best for products that look great on video and can be explained in a few seconds: beauty, gadgets, kitchen tools, fashion accessories, home organization and novelty items. Brands willing to work with creators, offer samples, and produce frequent content can reach large audiences quickly. Resellers and small brands who already make TikTok content also have an advantage.

It is a tougher fit for sellers who want to list and wait for search traffic, or whose products need lengthy explanation. Seller onboarding involves business verification and product review. One-off secondhand items can be sold in pre-owned categories, but the platform is mainly geared to products you can restock.

## What it costs to sell

TikTok charges a referral fee on each order. The official US category table lists 6% for most categories, 5% for certain jewelry sub-categories and pre-owned items, and 3% on the portion of a collectibles or pre-owned sale above $10,000. The fee is calculated on what the customer pays plus any platform-funded discount, minus tax, and it includes shipping paid by the customer.

Other costs to plan for:

- **Affiliate commissions** you agree to pay creators who promote your products.
- **Refund administration**, a fee equal to 20% of the referral fee on refunded orders, capped at $5 per SKU since May 15, 2025.
- **Fulfillment**, if you use FBT, plus advertising.

Some independent seller guides published in September 2026 report a rise to 8% for many categories from August 2026, but TikTok's own category table, checked in October 2026, still shows 6%. Confirm the rate shown in your Seller Center.

**Worked example ($50 item, free shipping, no affiliate, 6% rate):**

- Referral fee: 6% × $50 = $3.00
- You receive: $50.00 − $3.00 = **$47.00**, before shipping and product costs.
- With a 15% creator commission, add 15% × $50 = $7.50, leaving $39.50.
- At the reported 8% rate, the referral fee would be $4.00.

Fees verified October 2026 on TikTok Shop's official [referral fees by category page](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5988482086864682).

## Strengths

- **Built-in discovery.** Videos can reach viewers who were not searching for your product.
- **Creator network.** Affiliates can promote your products in exchange for commission.
- **Competitive base fee.** The 6% referral rate is lower than many big retail platforms.
- **Live selling.** Live streams let you demonstrate products and answer questions in real time.
- **Fulfillment option.** FBT reduces the logistics burden.

## Watch-outs

- **Content demands.** Sales depend on a steady stream of videos or creator posts.
- **Hidden cost stack.** Affiliate commissions, samples and ads can exceed the referral fee.
- **Fee uncertainty.** Reports of higher rates in 2026 mean you should verify your actual rate.
- **Strict shipping standards.** Late dispatch and cancellations can lead to penalties.
- **Policy changes.** Rules for categories and promotions shift frequently.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Lead every video with the product solving a problem in the first two seconds.
2. Set a clear affiliate commission rate and offer free samples to creators who already post in your niche.
3. Price with all costs in mind: referral fee, creator commission, shipping, returns and any ad spend.
4. Write product titles with the key search terms and add clear, bright images and size details.
5. Go live regularly to demo products and answer questions while viewers can buy instantly.
6. Ship within the required handling time and keep stock levels accurate to avoid penalties.
7. Track which videos and creators drive sales, then double down on what works.

## Buyer protection in brief

Orders placed through TikTok Shop are covered by its customer protections, which help buyers get refunds or returns if items do not arrive, arrive damaged, or are not as described. Buyers request help in the app, and TikTok can step in if the seller does not resolve the issue. Sellers should ship with tracking, describe products accurately and respond promptly to after-sales requests, since slow responses can result in automatic refunds.

Keep proof of what you ship, such as packing photos for higher-value orders, and make sure product claims in videos match the listing. Creator videos are part of what buyers rely on, so if an affiliate overstates a feature, the resulting returns can land on you. Giving creators accurate product briefs is part of managing your protection exposure.
