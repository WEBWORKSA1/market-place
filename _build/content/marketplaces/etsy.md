## At a glance

- Focused on handmade items, vintage goods (Etsy defines vintage by age) and craft supplies.
- Three core fees: a listing fee, a transaction fee and a payment processing fee.
- Listings last four months and cost $0.20 each time they are created or renewed.
- Offsite Ads can add a fee on sales that come from Etsy's external advertising.
- Customizable shop pages let you build a recognizable brand inside the platform.

## Who it's best for

Etsy is built for sellers whose products have a story: jewelry makers, illustrators, woodworkers, candle makers, knitters, print-on-demand designers who create original artwork, and vintage dealers. Buyers arrive expecting something they cannot find in a big-box store, which makes room for higher prices if your work stands out. It also works for digital downloads like printable planners and patterns.

It is not a good home for mass-produced resale goods, which conflict with Etsy's rules, or for sellers who only want a quick one-off sale.

## What it costs to sell

Etsy charges in three layers. First, a $0.20 listing fee every time you publish or renew a listing; if you sell several units from one listing, the renewal is charged per sale. Second, a 6.5% transaction fee on the price you set, plus the shipping and gift-wrap charges you collect (sales tax is excluded from this one for US sellers). Third, a payment processing fee through Etsy Payments, which for US-based shops is 3% plus $0.25 per order, calculated on the full order amount including shipping and tax.

There is one more fee to know about. If a buyer clicks one of Etsy's off-site ads and then buys from you, an Offsite Ads fee of 15% applies to that order. Shops that have made at least $10,000 in the past year pay 12% and cannot opt out; the fee is capped at $100 per order. Some countries also have a regulatory operating fee.

**Worked example ($50 item, free shipping, US shop, no Offsite Ads, no tax):**

- Listing fee: $0.20
- Transaction fee: 6.5% × $50 = $3.25
- Payment processing: (3% × $50) + $0.25 = $1.50 + $0.25 = $1.75
- Total fees: $0.20 + $3.25 + $1.75 = $5.20
- You receive: $50.00 − $5.20 = **$44.80**, before materials and shipping costs.

If that order came through an Offsite Ad, an extra 15% ($7.50) would apply.

Fees verified October 2026 on Etsy's official [fees and payments policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/) and [Etsy Payments policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/etsy-payments/).

## Strengths

- **Intent-driven buyers.** Shoppers come specifically for unique, handmade or vintage items.
- **Low cost to start.** A listing costs $0.20, so you can test products cheaply.
- **Brand-building tools.** Shop banners, about sections, and reviews help repeat buyers find you.
- **Global audience.** Etsy handles currency conversion, so international buyers can purchase easily.
- **Good for digital goods.** Instant downloads have no shipping cost at all.

## Watch-outs

- **Crowded search.** Popular niches, especially print-on-demand, are saturated.
- **Fee layering.** The listing, transaction, processing and possible Offsite Ads fees add up; the ads fee can surprise new sellers.
- **Rules on what counts as handmade.** Reselling factory goods can get listings removed or shops suspended.
- **Payout reserves.** New or fast-growing shops may have part of their funds held for a period.
- **Algorithm changes.** Search visibility can shift without notice.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Research the search terms buyers actually type and use them naturally in your title and all 13 tags.
2. Shoot photos in natural light and include a lifestyle image showing scale and use.
3. Price with every fee in mind, including the possible Offsite Ads fee, so a single ad-driven sale does not wipe out your margin.
4. Offer free US shipping on orders above a threshold you set; Etsy's search tends to favor it.
5. Fill out your shop's about section, policies and FAQ to build trust with first-time buyers.
6. Renew or add new listings regularly; fresh listings give you more chances to appear in search.
7. Ask happy buyers for reviews with a short follow-up message and package insert.

## Buyer protection in brief

Etsy's Purchase Protection program covers eligible orders that never arrive, arrive damaged, or do not match the listing. Buyers first contact the seller; if there is no fix, they can open a case and Etsy reviews it. For sellers who follow the rules, such as shipping on time with tracking and responding to messages, Etsy may refund the buyer itself rather than taking the money from the seller. That makes tracked shipping and prompt communication especially worthwhile.

Your shop policies also matter. Clear statements on returns, exchanges, custom orders and processing times set expectations before anyone buys, and they give Etsy something to reference if a case is opened. For personalized items, confirm spelling and details with the buyer in messages before you make anything, since those conversations become your record if a dispute arises later.
