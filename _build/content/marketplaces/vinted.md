## At a glance

- Secondhand clothing, shoes, accessories, kids' items and some home goods.
- Listing and selling are free for private sellers; you keep the agreed price.
- Buyers pay a Vinted fee, typically 5% of the item price plus a fixed €0.70 (or $0.70 on the US site).
- Buyers also pay shipping, usually through Vinted's integrated carriers.
- Optional paid promotions such as bumps and closet spotlights help visibility.

## Who it's best for

Vinted is a natural fit for people clearing out everyday wardrobes and children's clothes, where margins are small and every fee matters. Because sellers pay nothing to list or sell, it is one of the few platforms where a low-priced item still makes sense to post. Its largest audience is in Europe, and Vinted [announced its US expansion in January 2026](https://company.vinted.com/newsroom/vinted-launches-us-nyc-2), so US sellers are joining a newer, still-growing community.

It is less suited to high-end luxury or professional resellers who need business tools, since Vinted's pro seller program has separate terms. Parents, students and anyone refreshing a wardrobe each season tend to get the most out of it.

## What it costs to sell

For private sellers, Vinted is free: there is no listing fee and no commission on sales. When an item sells, the full agreed price is credited to your Vinted balance. The platform earns money from buyers instead. On "Buy now" orders, the buyer pays a Vinted fee, usually 5% of the item price plus a fixed €0.70 on European sites ($0.70 on the US site), excluding shipping and taxes. The exact fee can vary by item, order value and whether it is a bundle.

Shipping is paid by the buyer. Your only optional costs are promotional tools: bumps (3 or 7 days of extra visibility) and closet spotlight. Their prices are shown at checkout and depend on factors such as the item price.

**Worked example ($50 item, US site):**

- Seller fee: $0.00
- You receive: **$50.00**.
- The buyer pays the item price plus a fee of (5% × $50) + $0.70 = $2.50 + $0.70 = $3.20, for $53.20 before shipping and any tax.

In euro markets the same math applies: a €50 item costs the buyer €53.20 plus postage, and the seller receives €50.

Fees verified October 2026 on Vinted's official [pricelist](https://www.vinted.ie/pricelist) and the US [pricelist](https://www.vinted.com/pricelist).

## Strengths

- **No seller fees.** You keep the entire sale price.
- **Great for cheap items.** Low-value clothing still makes sense to sell.
- **Integrated shipping.** Labels and drop-off points are built into the app in supported countries.
- **Large European audience.** Strong reach in many European markets.
- **Simple listing.** Photos, a few details and a price are enough.

## Watch-outs

- **Buyers feel the fee.** The checkout fee makes listings look more expensive than the price tag, which can encourage haggling.
- **Low price expectations.** Many buyers come looking for bargains, which limits what you can charge for mid-range brands.
- **Newer in the US.** The American audience is smaller than in Europe, at least for now.
- **Promotion costs.** Bumps are optional, but heavy use reduces the zero-fee advantage.
- **Private seller rules.** Running a business through a personal account breaks the terms; business sellers must use the pro program.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Price competitively against similar listings; Vinted buyers compare closely and expect value.
2. Take photos in daylight against a plain background and include the label and any flaws.
3. Fill in brand, size, condition and color accurately so your item appears in filtered searches.
4. Turn on bundle discounts so buyers can combine items and save on postage.
5. Reply to offers quickly; a modest counteroffer often closes the sale.
6. Ship promptly once the label is generated, as delays can lead to cancellations.
7. Use a bump on a few items when you first list them to gain early views, then judge whether it is worth repeating.

## Buyer protection in brief

Vinted's buyer protection, funded by the checkout fee, covers orders paid through the platform. If an item does not arrive, arrives damaged, or is significantly not as described, the buyer can report it within a short window after delivery, and payment is held until the order is completed. Sellers are protected when they follow the rules, such as shipping with the provided label and describing items accurately. Payments or deals made outside Vinted are not covered.

Sellers can strengthen their position by packing photos, clear condition notes and quick shipping. If a buyer raises an issue, reply through the order conversation and share evidence, since Vinted reviews those messages when deciding a case. Never accept a request to pay outside the app or to click a link sent in chat, as these are common phishing tactics.
