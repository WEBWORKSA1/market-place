## At a glance

- Canadian local classifieds covering goods, vehicles, real estate, jobs, services and pets.
- No commission on sales; payment happens directly between buyer and seller.
- Most private listings in Buy & Sell are free.
- Some categories, such as vehicles beyond a free limit, tickets, services and real estate, charge per-listing fees.
- Paid ad features can increase visibility.

## Who it's best for

Kijiji is a go-to site for Canadians who want to sell locally without paying commission. It handles everything from furniture, appliances, sporting goods and baby gear to used cars and recreational vehicles, and it is available in English and French. It is especially handy for bulky items and for buyers who want to inspect goods before paying.

It is not built for nationwide shipping, so sellers wanting to reach buyers across the country should consider a shipping-focused platform.

Kijiji is also a common first stop for private vehicle sales in Canada. The first two ads in each vehicle group are free, which covers most households selling a car, a trailer or a boat. Movers clearing out a home before a relocation often lean on it too, because furniture and appliances can sell quickly to nearby buyers who bring their own truck.

## What it costs to sell

For most private sellers posting household goods in Buy & Sell, Kijiji is free. There is no commission taken from your sale, and Kijiji does not handle payment. Fees apply only in certain categories, and they are charged per listing for each 31-day period.

According to Kijiji's [category pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141), private sellers get no free allowance in categories like tickets, services, automotive services and pet adoption listings, while categories such as real estate for sale allow one free listing. In Cars & Vehicles, Kijiji's help pages describe a small flat fee once you post a third ad in the same vehicle group. Kijiji does not publish all prices on those pages; the exact amount is shown in CAD at the time of posting. Business accounts may face fees in additional categories, and frequent sellers can be classified as dealers.

**Worked example ($50 item, private seller, Buy & Sell, sold in person):**

- Kijiji fee: $0.00
- You receive: **$50.00 CAD**.

Optional visibility upgrades, if you buy them, are an extra cost you choose.

Fees verified October 2026 on Kijiji's official [category pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141) and [vehicle listing fee help page](https://help.kijiji.ca/basics/cars-vehicles-listing-fee).

## Strengths

- **No commission.** You keep the full sale price.
- **Strong Canadian presence.** Well known across the country, in both official languages.
- **Broad categories.** Goods, vehicles, rentals, services and jobs in one place.
- **Good for bulky items.** Local pickup avoids shipping costs entirely.
- **Simple posting.** Free ads in common categories take minutes.

## Watch-outs

- **No transaction protection.** Kijiji is not part of the payment and does not resolve disputes.
- **Scams.** Fake e-transfer notices, overpayment offers and shipping requests from distant "buyers" are red flags.
- **Fees in some categories.** Vehicle, ticket, service and real estate listings may cost money.
- **Listing expiry.** Ads have limited active periods and need reposting.
- **Dealer classification.** Frequent sellers can lose free ad allowances.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Choose the most precise category and location so your ad shows up in local searches.
2. Use several sharp photos, starting with a clear shot of the entire item.
3. Write a title with brand, item type and a key detail, such as size or model.
4. Mention your preferred payment method and pickup area in the description to reduce back-and-forth.
5. Repost or refresh ads when they slip down the results, following Kijiji's rules.
6. Verify any Interac e-Transfer in your banking app, not from an email, before releasing the item.
7. Meet in a public place for smaller items, and bring someone along for in-home pickups.

## Buyer protection in brief

Kijiji does not provide buyer or seller protection for local transactions, since it does not process payments or guarantee items. Buyers should inspect goods before paying, and sellers should confirm payment has arrived in their bank account before handing anything over. Suspicious users and ads can be reported to Kijiji, but recovering money from a bad deal is generally not something the platform can do.

For higher-value sales, a few extra steps help. Many police services in Canada offer exchange zones in their parking lots, which are a sensible meeting place. For vehicles, use a written bill of sale and follow your province's ownership transfer process. Keep the conversation in Kijiji's messaging until you are comfortable, and be cautious with anyone who asks you to click a link to "receive" money.
