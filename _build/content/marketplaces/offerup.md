## At a glance

- Mobile-first local classifieds for goods, vehicles, jobs and services.
- No fees or commission on in-person transactions.
- Nationwide shipping was retired in fall 2025, so new listings are local pickup only.
- Sellers get a monthly allotment of free listings; extra listings cost money.
- Optional promotions push listings higher in the local feed.

## Who it's best for

OfferUp is best for selling things you would rather not ship: furniture, appliances, bikes, tools, gaming consoles, phones and household goods. Because it is focused on local deals and does not take a commission, it is a straightforward way to sell to people nearby for cash or instant payment. Small businesses and service providers can also use its job and service listings.

It is also a reasonable choice for sellers who already post on other local apps. Since OfferUp charges nothing on in-person sales, listing the same couch or bike here adds more nearby buyers at no extra cost, and the first person to show up with payment wins. Just remember to remove the listing everywhere once it sells.

Sellers who want to reach buyers across the country should look elsewhere. According to OfferUp's own [help article on the shipping change](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/41043186552980-Nationwide-shipping-ending), new items could no longer be posted with shipping from September 23, 2025, and shipping was removed from remaining eligible listings on October 21, 2025.

## What it costs to sell

For ordinary sellers, OfferUp is free to use. There is no commission on in-person sales, because payment happens directly between you and the buyer, outside the platform. You get a monthly allowance of free listings; if you post more than that, additional listings carry a fee shown in the app before you pay.

Optional paid features include promotions that place your item higher in the local feed, plus dedicated listings for jobs and services. These are billed through the app stores, and prices are shown before purchase.

**Worked example ($50 item, sold in person):**

- OfferUp fee: $0.00
- You receive: **$50.00** in cash or via your preferred payment app.

If you pay to promote the listing, that cost comes out of your pocket separately.

Fees verified October 2026 on OfferUp's official help article [What is OfferUp?](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031989092-What-is-OfferUp).

## Strengths

- **No commission.** Every dollar of the sale price is yours.
- **Great for bulky items.** No packaging or shipping hassle.
- **Simple app.** Listing takes a minute, and chat is built in.
- **Public meetup suggestions.** The app points users toward safer, well-lit meeting spots.
- **Vehicles, jobs and services.** Broader than a pure goods marketplace.

## Watch-outs

- **Local only.** Your audience is limited to people within driving distance.
- **No platform protection on cash deals.** If something goes wrong in person, OfferUp is not a party to the transaction.
- **Scams.** Fake payment confirmations, overpayment schemes and requests to move to text are common warning signs.
- **No-shows and haggling.** Buyers often negotiate at the meetup or fail to appear.
- **Listing limits.** Heavy sellers may need to pay for extra listings.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Take a bright, uncluttered cover photo; buyers decide quickly from small thumbnails.
2. State condition, dimensions and pickup area clearly to cut down on repetitive questions.
3. Price slightly above your minimum, since in-person buyers almost always negotiate.
4. Reply fast and propose a specific time and public meeting place, ideally one the app suggests.
5. For valuable items, ask for payment before handing over the item and verify it has actually arrived in your account.
6. Bring a way to demonstrate electronics work, such as a charged battery or a power source, to close the deal on the spot.
7. Try a short promotion on higher-value items in competitive categories, and skip it for things that sell quickly anyway.

## Buyer protection in brief

Because OfferUp no longer handles shipping and in-person payments happen outside the platform, there is no general purchase guarantee for local deals. Protection comes from your own precautions: meet in public, inspect items before paying, use cash or a payment method you trust, and avoid sharing personal financial details. Users can rate each other and report suspicious accounts, which helps the community, but it does not replace caution.

Check a buyer's profile and ratings before agreeing to meet, and be wary of brand-new accounts that push for urgency. Agree on the price in chat before meeting so there are fewer surprises. For phones and electronics, reset the device in front of the buyer once payment is complete. If anything feels off, it is fine to cancel the meetup; another buyer will usually come along.
