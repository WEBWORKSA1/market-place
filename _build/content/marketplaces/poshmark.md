## At a glance

- Focused on clothing, shoes, accessories, beauty and home decor.
- Simple two-tier fee: $2.95 on small sales, 20% on everything over $15.
- The buyer pays for a prepaid shipping label, so you do not handle postage quotes.
- Social features such as sharing, following and "Posh Parties" drive visibility.
- Higher-value items can go through an authentication step before reaching the buyer.

## Who it's best for

Poshmark is designed for fashion. It works well for people clearing out a closet, for resellers who specialize in mall and designer brands, and for sellers who like a community feel and do not mind spending time sharing listings. Because the buyer pays for shipping at a flat rate, shipping is simple and predictable for clothing and shoes.

It is less attractive for low-margin items, electronics or anything bulky, and the 20% commission makes it one of the costlier options for mid-priced goods.

The flat $2.95 fee on small sales changes the math at the low end. A $10 top costs you $2.95, which is almost 30% of the price, while a $15 sale nets $12.05 and a $16 sale nets $12.80. Below $15 the flat fee eats a large share of each sale, so sellers often avoid listing very cheap pieces on their own, and bundle cheaper items so the buyer pays a single shipping label for several pieces.

## What it costs to sell

Poshmark's US fee is easy to calculate. For a sale of $15 or less, you pay a flat $2.95. For a sale over $15, Poshmark keeps 20% of the sale price. These rates came back into effect on October 24, 2024, after a brief experiment with a different structure, and the buyer protection fee that was part of that experiment was dropped.

Shipping is generally paid by the buyer, so it does not come out of your earnings unless you choose to offer a shipping discount, in which case the discount is deducted from your payout. There are no listing fees.

**Worked example ($50 item, buyer pays shipping):**

- Commission: 20% × $50 = $10.00
- You receive: $50.00 − $10.00 = **$40.00**.

**Small sale ($12 item):** fee $2.95, you receive $12.00 − $2.95 = $9.05.

Fees verified October 2026 on Poshmark's official [legal center fee notice](https://poshmark.com/fee_policy). Canadian rates differ.

## Strengths

- **Predictable fees.** No processing charges or listing fees to track.
- **Buyer-paid shipping.** The label is generated for you and the buyer covers it.
- **Engaged fashion audience.** Shoppers come specifically for clothing and accessories.
- **Social discovery.** Sharing and following help listings get seen without paid ads.
- **Offers to likers.** You can send discounted offers to people who liked an item.

## Watch-outs

- **High commission.** At 20%, mid-priced items cost more to sell than on most rivals.
- **Time investment.** Visibility often depends on sharing your closet regularly.
- **Lowball offers.** Many buyers expect to negotiate below your list price.
- **Shipping discounts cost you.** Offering reduced shipping comes out of your earnings.
- **Fashion-centric.** Items outside fashion and home have a smaller audience here.

## Tips to sell more here

1. Price a little above your target to leave room for offers, which are expected on this platform.
2. Use a clean, bright cover photo; a flat lay or modeled shot usually beats a hanger photo.
3. Include brand, size, style and color in the title and fill in every listing field so filters find you.
4. Share your closet in short sessions daily, and join relevant themed parties to reach active shoppers.
5. Send "offer to likers" with a discount once an item has a few likes, and pair it with a shipping discount on higher-priced items.
6. Bundle-friendly pricing helps; encourage buyers to combine items to save on a single shipping label.
7. Ship within a day or two with neat packaging; prompt shipping earns better ratings and repeat buyers.

## Buyer protection in brief

Poshmark's Posh Protect program holds payment until the buyer receives the order and accepts it, or the short review window ends. If an item is not as described, damaged, or does not arrive, the buyer can open a case, and Poshmark decides the outcome. Sellers are also covered against certain issues, such as packages lost in transit when using the prepaid label. Detailed photos and honest descriptions of flaws are the best defense against returns.

Measurements are especially useful in fashion, where fit complaints are common. Listing pit-to-pit width, length and inseam lets buyers check fit before they purchase, and gives Poshmark clear information to rely on if a "not as described" case is opened. Keep the original tags or a photo of them for branded items, as these help confirm authenticity.
