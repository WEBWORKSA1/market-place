## 2. Must-have features (prioritised)

1. **True-fee calculator for every major platform.** Inputs: sale price, shipping charged, item cost, seller region, category. Output: each fee line (listing, transaction/final value, payment processing, per-order, promoted/boost), net payout, margin, and a side-by-side ranking across platforms. Swappa, Reverb and Crosslist each do a narrow version; nobody independent does it across 20+ platforms in one view.
2. **Per-platform fee pages** (one URL each, e.g. `/fees/etsy/`) with a dated fee table, a worked example, a "last verified" stamp linking to the official source, and a change log. Etsy, Mercari and Depop all stamp effective dates; we should go further and keep history.
3. **Filterable marketplace directory** (category, region, model, fee range, "best for"). Cards with logo, one-line verdict, fee summary and official link.
4. **Head-to-head comparison pages** ("Poshmark vs Mercari", "Etsy vs Amazon Handmade") — Vendoo and Crosslist use these for SEO, but theirs are sales pages for their own tool.
5. **Scam & safety hub** for buyers and sellers: scam encyclopedia, red-flag checklists, safe-meetup guidance, payment-method risk table, "what each platform's protection actually covers" table.
6. **Seller guides**: listing photos, titles/SEO, pricing from sold comps, shipping and packing, taxes (marketplace-facilitator rules), crosslisting.
7. **Lead desk** ("Get matched with the right marketplace" / "Launch your own marketplace") with a multi-step form.
8. **Newsletter** with a weekly "fee & policy changes" roundup — the single most defensible recurring content (Value Added Resource proves demand).
9. **Video library** of vetted third-party explainers (FTC, Etsy, Shopify, Sharetribe), embedded on the relevant guides.
10. **Contests / community** (e.g. "best listing photo of the month") and a sponsor slot, added after traffic exists.

## 3. Lead-generation patterns that convert

- **Free valuation / assessment as the lead magnet.** Flippa ("Takes 5 minutes. No obligation."), Acquire ("Free SaaS valuation") and Empire Flippers all lead with a free estimate. Our equivalent: **"Free Marketplace Fit Check"** — answer 6 questions, get a ranked shortlist of platforms plus an offer of a human review.
- **Dual CTA: high intent + low commitment.** Rakuten pairs "Apply" with "Request materials" in every section. We should pair "Book a consultation" with "Get the free checklist / fit report".
- **Multi-step form, easy questions first.** Recommended order: (1) what you sell (tiles), (2) where you sell now (multi-select logos), (3) monthly sales band (radio), (4) target regions, (5) goal (choose platform / grow sales / launch own marketplace), (6) name + email + optional website. Show a progress bar; put contact fields last.
- **Pre-qualification shown up front.** Empire Flippers lists minimum profit and history before signup; Walmart lists document requirements. A short "who we can help" block filters out poor leads and raises trust.
- **Trust signals beside the form:** response-time promise, "no obligation", privacy note ("we never sell your details"), List Perfectly-style pledge ("we never ask for your marketplace passwords"), named reviewer with photo, independence statement (no platform pays for rankings).
- **CTA copy patterns that recur on high-converting pages:** outcome-first verbs ("Get a free valuation", "See what you'll really earn", "Find your best marketplace"), time cost ("2 minutes"), and risk reversal ("No obligation", "Free").
- **Ungated tools.** Jungle Scout gates its calculator behind an email form and sets the page to noindex. For an AdSense site, keep calculators open (traffic and ad impressions) and offer an optional "email me this breakdown" capture after the result.
- **Exit points on every guide:** a contextual inline box ("Selling more than $5k/month? Get a free channel review") rather than generic popups.

## 4. Trust & safety content patterns

- **Reassure first, then be specific.** Poshmark leads with "you are protected", then gives exact deadlines; Kijiji ends its reporting guide by easing worry about false reports. Our pages should open calmly and then give concrete numbers.
- **Explicit "not covered" lists.** Poshmark's exclusions (fit, change of mind, off-platform trades) are the most useful part of the page. A cross-platform table — *protection window, what's covered, what isn't, who pays return shipping* — is a content asset no marketplace will publish for competitors.
- **"Stay on platform" message.** Poshmark and Vinted both tie protection to paying in-app. This is the number-one anti-scam rule and should appear on every buyer guide.
- **Third-party trust badges.** Back Market shows Trustpilot, BBB and B Corp; Acquire shows a licence number. For us: editorial policy, named author bios, "last verified" dates and source links on every fee claim.
- **Authority embeds.** FTC and BBB publish short scam videos; embedding them (with credit) adds authority and dwell time.
- **Step-wise reporting guides** (Kijiji): where the report button lives, which reason to pick, what happens next.
- **Disclosure language.** Gumtree's plain statement that commissions don't raise the user's price is a good template for our affiliate/sponsor disclosure.

## 5. Design & UX patterns

- **Navigation:** mega-menu grouped by job-to-be-done (Walmart: Launch / Ship / Grow). Proposed top nav: Compare · Fee Calculator · Directory · Sell Smarter · Buy Safer · Get Help. Sticky header with one persistent CTA (Vendoo).
- **Hero:** one claim + one tool. Gumroad shows two big numbers; Flippa shows a search plus a valuation CTA. Our hero should put the fee calculator (price input + platform picker) directly above the fold.
- **Filters:** Flippa's price-band buckets, Kijiji/Wallapop quick-filter chips, OfferUp's "Nearby". For the directory: chips for category, region and model, plus a fee-range slider; filter state in the URL hash so filtered views are shareable.
- **Cards:** logo, name, one-line verdict, three facts (fee, regions, model), "Official site" and "Full review" links. Flippa's metric-dense cards and OLX's minimal cards show the range; keep ours scannable.
- **Comparison tables:** Amazon's two-plan cards and Swappa's competitor payout example. Use sticky first column, highlight the cheapest result, and show "verified on [date]".
- **Calculators:** Shopify's slider plus collapsible "Advanced" fields with a "why we ask this" tooltip; Crosslist's two-field minimum. Show results live (no submit button), with a fee breakdown bar and a copy/share link.
- **Help-article layout:** Etsy/eBay jump-link table of contents, "Top takeaway" summary box, worked examples, "Was this helpful?" widget.
- **Social proof blocks:** big-number stat strips (Acquire, Vendoo) — only use numbers we can stand behind.
- **Accessibility:** skip-to-content links (Walmart, Kijiji, 1stDibs); never disable zoom (TikTok Shop Academy does); dark mode toggle (Value Added Resource).
- **Location/currency selector** (Back Market, Etsy): a region switch that re-runs fee calculations in local currency.

## 6. Content gaps Market.Place can own

1. **Cross-platform true-fee comparison.** Every marketplace explains only its own fees, often across several pages (Whatnot and StockX route users to other articles). Tool vendors build single-platform calculators to sell subscriptions. An independent, all-platforms-in-one calculator does not exist in a credible form.
2. **Fee change history.** Mercari, Depop and Etsy have all changed fees recently with effective dates buried in footnotes. A public, dated "fee change tracker" per platform is linkable, newsworthy and repeatable.
3. **Protection-policy comparison** (what's covered, windows, exclusions) across resale and classifieds platforms.
4. **Region-specific guidance** for Canada, UK, India, EU and Latin America (Kijiji, Gumtree, OLX, Vinted, Wallapop, Mercado Libre) — most English-language content is US-only.
5. **"Which marketplace should I sell X on?"** item-level guides (sneakers, guitars, vintage furniture, phones, handmade jewellery) built from the directory data.
6. **Launching your own marketplace** for non-technical founders — Sharetribe covers it but is a vendor; a neutral guide comparing build options, take rates and costs is open ground and feeds the lead desk.
7. **Buyer-side safety** across platforms in one place, with official video embeds.
8. **Business/domain marketplace comparison** (Flippa vs Acquire vs Empire Flippers vs Sedo): fee models differ wildly (listing packages, tiered commission, subscriptions) and nobody compares them neutrally.

## 7. AdSense-approval checklist observations

- **Substantial original content before applying:** aim for 25–40 in-depth pages (fee pages, guides, comparisons) of 800+ words each, not just tools. Calculator-only pages are thin; pair each tool with explanation, worked examples and an FAQ (the Shopify and Jungle Scout pattern).
- **Required trust pages:** About (who runs the site and why), Contact (working form/email), Privacy Policy (cookies, AdSense/Google partner disclosure), Terms, Editorial & Fee-Verification Policy, Affiliate/Advertising Disclosure. All major sites here link legal pages from the footer.
- **Cookie consent:** Etsy shows a consent modal with categories; for EU/UK/Canada visitors use a Google-certified CMP before AdSense loads.
- **No gated or noindex content** on pages you want monetised (Jungle Scout's calculator page is noindex).
- **Clear navigation and no dead ends:** every page reachable from the nav or footer, breadcrumbs on deep pages, an HTML sitemap and `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and `ads.txt` at the root.
- **Accuracy signals:** "last verified" dates and links to the official fee page next to every fee figure; never state an unverified fee (the directory uses "See official fee page" where we could not confirm).
- **No trademark confusion:** follow eRank and Vendoo — add a footer disclaimer that marketplace names and logos belong to their owners and Market.Place is not affiliated.
- **Ad placement restraint:** keep ads away from calculator inputs and form buttons (accidental-click risk); use in-content and sidebar units on guides, a single unit below calculator results.
- **Performance and mobile:** static pages, compressed images, no layout shift from late ad slots (reserve ad space with fixed min-heights).
- **Original writing only:** do not paraphrase platform help pages closely; add our own examples, comparisons and data.
