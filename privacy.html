Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated 2026-10-31

This policy explains what information Market.Place ("we") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and your choices. Market.Place is an independent informational website.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (Growth Desk, contact, newsletter, support pledge, contest entry, job application or advertising inquiry), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to respond, provide what you asked for, run contests and send the newsletter if you subscribed. Form messages are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit), which processes the data only to deliver it.

We never sell or rent your personal information. Growth Desk requests may be shared with one vetted partner only if you agree first.

Calculators and tools

Our calculators, quiz and checkers run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us or stored on our servers. Some tools put your inputs in the page address so you can share a result, and that address stays on your device unless you share it.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (light or dark) and your cookie choice. Our host and font provider may log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance.

Advertising: Google AdSense

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice, we ask Google to show non-personalized ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be shown a Google-certified consent message, as required.

Embedded videos

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play. After that, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use the contact form with the topic "Privacy request". You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your request, or as the law requires. The site is served over HTTPS.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest version.
